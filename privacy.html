Privacy Policy

Shot Tracker (bkgstats.com and the Shot Tracker app) · Effective 24 November 2026

In short: we keep only what the app needs to track your golf: your account, your rounds and your shots. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and all of it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, your display name and your password. The password is stored only in hashed form by our account provider, so we can't read it.
  • Your golf: the rounds you play: course, tees, date, scores, and each shot you log (club, lie, result and where it was on the map). Also your settings, such as handicap comparison, average drive and the clubs in your bag.
  • Your location, only while you use it: while you play a hole, the app uses your phone's location to show distances and to place shots. Only the shot positions you save are kept. Your location isn't tracked in the background and isn't stored as a trail.
  • Coaching: if you use the coaching features, we store the link between you and your coach, the messages and notifications you exchange, and any edits a coach makes to your rounds.

If you use the app without logging in, your rounds stay only on your device, in the browser's or app's own storage.

How we use it

  • To save your rounds and show them on every device you log in on.
  • To work out your stats and strokes gained.
  • To let the coaches you choose see and help with your game.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Who can see it

  • You. Your rounds are private to your account.
  • Your coaches. A coach on your roster can see and edit your rounds.
  • Anyone with a live link you share. If you share a round live, anyone you give the link to can watch that round. Nobody can find it without the link.

Services the app relies on

  • Supabase stores accounts and synced data.
  • Esri provides the satellite imagery. Your device downloads the map pictures straight from Esri.
  • Open-Meteo provides the wind. The app sends it the course's location, nothing about you.
  • EmailJS sends the email when you ask to become a coach. It receives your name and email for that message.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and YouTube serve fonts, a spreadsheet reader for club imports, and drill videos.

As with any website, these services see your device's IP address when your device fetches something from them.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept while you have an account.
  • You can delete a single round in the app at any time.
  • To delete your account, log in and go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything saved to it, straight away. Your account is shared with the coaching app, so it is deleted there too.
  • To keep a copy first, use Settings → Offline imagery & backup → Download backup.

Children

Shot Tracker isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
